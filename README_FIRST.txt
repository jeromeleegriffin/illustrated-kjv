AUTHORIZED KJV BIBLE TRIVIA & STUDY APP — RECOVERY REVISION

START HERE
The app opens with the entire Authorized King James Bible.
Strong’s word links are blue; Bullinger note links are yellow.
Bible Trivia, Search, and Entire Bible / Reading progress are controls inside the app.
Click a verse to highlight it and mark it read; use Bookmark this verse to save your place.
The reading overview grays out read verses and shows how many remain.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- All 5,000 original trivia questions and existing progress behavior.
- All 66 books and 31,102 Authorized KJV verses; the scripture words are preserved.
- Repaired Bible search, accessible on phones and desktops.
- 23,934 Bullinger Companion Bible note records in a separate study panel with yellow links.
- All 198 numbered appendices, two supplements, and 49 diagrams.
- Locally bundled Strong’s word tags and 14,197 dictionary entries.
- Existing illustrations and Genesis contextual study suggestions.

RUN ON WINDOWS
Extract the ZIP. Open a terminal in The_Illustrated_KJV and run:
  py -m http.server 8087 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory dist
Then open:
  http://localhost:8087

FOR GROK
The public app files are in dist/. Keep the entire folder and its subfolders together.
All links and data paths support a hosted subfolder. This package has not been
pushed or deployed to GitHub. Source provenance, import scripts, test reports,
and actual screen captures are included for review.

READ THE LIMITS
Eight Bullinger source records have unresolved verse numbers and four are
Psalm title notes. They are retained at chapter level with clear labels.
The complete printed book introductions and every structural diagram have
not been certified against original scans. README.md provides exact scope,
source links, licensing attribution, and verification details.
