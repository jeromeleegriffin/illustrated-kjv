THE ILLUSTRATED KING JAMES BIBLE

OPEN THE READER
For everyday reading, open:
https://the-illustrated-kjv.jeromeleegriffin.chatgpt.site

The reader covers all 66 books and 31,102 KJV verses. Select a book and chapter, search the KJV, or tap a dotted word to open its Hebrew or Greek Strong’s entry. Each entry includes a contextual reading suggestion and a direct BibleHub link. The reading includes original vector context illustrations at selected passages across the Old and New Testaments.

KJV WORDING
The scripture is displayed from the local KJV source file, unchanged by notes or illustrations. The original source includes 66 books and 31,102 verses; Genesis 1:1 was checked against the familiar KJV wording.

STRONG’S LINKS
Full KJV word-to-Strong’s alignment and lexicon data load from jsonBible.org when a passage or word is opened. An internet connection is required for those word links. Genesis also includes 123 selective contextual notes from the earlier Genesis study draft. Strong’s definitions show a word’s broad lexical range, not every possible sense in each verse. Context suggestions are interpretations to compare, not authoritative replacements for the scripture.

LOCAL COPY
The site files are in the `dist` folder. To run the copy locally on Windows, install/use Python, open a terminal in the `dist` folder, and run:

  py -m http.server 8000

Then open http://localhost:8000 in a browser. Keep an internet connection available for Strong’s entries. The hosted reader is simpler to open on a phone.
